Just type.

A Windows typing assistant that fixes your typos as you go, finishes your sentences, and learns how you write. Everything runs on your own computer.

Download for Windows Free and open source · per-user install, no admin · Windows 11

Fixes typos on the space bar

thhe becomes the the instant you finish the word. A brief blue underline shows what moved; ← within 3 seconds undoes it.

Finishes your sentences

Pause and grey ghost text completes the sentence. Tab accepts it and offers the next one. Ctrl+Tab takes one word.

Learns how you write

Retype a word and the pair becomes an autocorrect entry. Undo a fix twice and the word joins your dictionary. Rules you keep undoing stop auto-applying.

Works everywhere

Mail, chat, browsers, editors, terminals. Built with Claude Code's prompt in mind.

Fast

About 120 ms from keystroke to suggestion on an NVIDIA GPU, with Gemma 3 1B. Runs on CPU with SmolLM2.

Insights

A tray flyout with today's numbers, and a dashboard with acceptance over time, latency, per-app stats, and what the model saw.

Everything stays on your computer.

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. The only network calls are the first-run downloads of the engine and the model.

  • Card numbers, SSNs, IBANs, account numbers and password: secrets never reach the model.
  • Banking and health sites are off by default, and the list is yours to edit.
  • Delete one folder and it forgets everything.
